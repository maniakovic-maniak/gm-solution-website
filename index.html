Product Features

Formula-level recalculation

For models that fit within a single pass, every formula gets read in full - not sampled. Catches the errors a values-only review structurally can't: a discount-rate mismatch, a metric wired to the wrong line, logic that's technically valid but economically wrong.

Domain-specific review

The review adapts to what you upload. Mining models get checked against cut-off grade economics and royalty structures. Property models against development and GDV logic. Infrastructure against concession and project-finance mechanics - automatically, no manual setup.

A real review deliverable

Not a bug list. A structured, multi-tab report - issue log, validation matrix, remediation plan, scope and reliance - the same shape a client-facing review deliverable actually takes.

Grounded in real standards

Every check maps back to an established methodology - the FAST Standard and ICAEW's Financial Modelling Code - not an ad-hoc list of gut checks.

Works from your phone

Upload a model and check on a run from a mobile browser - no desktop required to kick off a review or see where it stands.

Delivered to your inbox

No need to keep a tab open. The report lands in your client's email the moment it's ready, so a review can run in the background of your day.

Security

Isolated Infrastructure

Runs on dedicated, root-controlled infrastructure - never shared hosting. Each run executes in its own isolated, purpose-provisioned environment.

Encryption Everywhere

Every connection runs over TLS end to end. Uploaded models are encrypted at rest with AES-256, not stored as plain files on disk.

Data Residency

Infrastructure is hosted in-region, so your model never has to leave the jurisdiction your business operates in.

Not Used For Training

Your model is used to generate your review - nothing else. It is never used to train PlsFx's models or any third party's.

Scheduled Deletion

Uploaded files and working data are cleared automatically on a retention sweep - nothing lingers by default once your review is done.

Payment Security

Billing runs through a dedicated payment processor. Card details are never handled or stored directly by PlsFx.

Every major decision deserves a model that's actually been checked!

Financial model review hasn't kept pace with what's actually possible.

Every major decision - a loan, an investment, a board sign-off - sits on top of a spreadsheet nobody outside the room who built it has genuinely checked, because doing it properly has always been slow, expensive, and dependent on one specialist's calendar.

That gap exists for two reasons.

First, most tools that claim to "check" a model only look at what it outputs, not how it gets there. A values-only glance can't catch a discount rate wired to the wrong cell, or a metric that's technically correct but economically meaningless - catching that means tracing the formula logic itself.

Second, a review is only as good as the standard behind it. A mining model and a property development model don't fail in the same places - generic checklists miss what's specific to each.

PlsFx closes both gaps at once.

We recalculate the model's actual formula logic, not just its outputs, catching what a surface-level pass structurally can't see - grounded in a domain-specific methodology built on standards like the FAST Standard and ICAEW's Financial Modelling Code, not gut-feel checks.

The result is a structured, multi-tab report - the same shape a client-facing deliverable actually takes - in minutes instead of weeks, at a fraction of the cost.

Where we're going. This is the beginning of what automated model review can look like. We're expanding industry coverage, deepening how much of a model we can verify in one pass, and building toward the kind of institutional-grade review today's process was never built to support.

Our goal is to be the standard financial teams reach for by default - not a workaround for when a specialist isn't available, but a genuinely better way to know a model is right.
